SERVICE / Attack Surface Validation

Don't count vulnerabilities. Validate attack paths.

Scanners list issues; attackers chain them. OSEC.uz maps your external and internal surface, tests which exposures are reachable and exploitable, and shows the path an attacker would take.

What we test

  • External perimeter and internet-facing assets
  • Application and API exposure
  • Internal network and trust boundaries
  • Cloud, configuration and infrastructure exposure

What you get

  • An inventory of what is actually exposed
  • Exploitability tested, not assumed
  • Findings ranked by attack path, not by scanner score
  • A route to remediation your engineers can act on
