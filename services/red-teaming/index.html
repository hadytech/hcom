SERVICE / Red Teaming

Red teaming against a real adversary model.

A red team assessment asks one question: could an attacker reach what matters to you, and would you notice? OSEC.uz emulates realistic adversary tradecraft against agreed objectives and reports what worked, what was detected, and what to change.

What we test

  • Objective-based adversary emulation
  • Initial access and lateral movement
  • Endpoint detection and response (EDR) evasion research
  • Identity and Active Directory abuse
  • Detection and response gap analysis

What you get

  • Rules of engagement and objectives defined up front
  • Attack narrative mapped to MITRE ATT&CK
  • Findings tied to detection opportunities
  • A debrief with your defenders

From our research

  • Bypassing EDR syscalls
  • Active Directory attack paths
